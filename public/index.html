Add it once

Then let the calendar do the work.

Subscribe instead of downloading. New showtimes and schedule changes will appear automatically.

1

Copy the link

Use the button below to copy the calendar subscription address.

2

Open your calendar

Look for “Add calendar by URL” or “New calendar subscription.”

3

Paste and subscribe

Choose an automatic refresh interval and you’re all set.

Apple Calendar

On Mac, choose File → New Calendar Subscription. On iPhone or iPad, open Settings → Apps → Calendar → Calendar Accounts → Add Account → Other → Add Subscribed Calendar.

Google Calendar

On a computer, open Google Calendar. Beside Other calendars, choose + → From URL, then paste the subscription link.

Outlook

Open the calendar, choose Add calendar → Subscribe from web, paste the subscription link, and give the calendar a name.
